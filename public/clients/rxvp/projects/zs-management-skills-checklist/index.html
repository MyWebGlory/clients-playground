ZS Emerging Leaders Management Skills Checklist

Please select the top five skills the participant should strengthen.

Time Management and Prioritization

  • Maintains focus on end goals without getting distracted by minor issues
  • Prioritizes work deliverables and provides solutions
  • Sets clear expectations for individual work output
  • Connects day-to-day work to broader business objectives

Skills for Participants with Direct Reports

  • Sets clear expectations for teamwork output
  • Effectively delegate work
  • Holds team members accountable

Communication Skills

  • Contributes strongly to brainstorming discussions
  • Is comfortable presenting an opposing opinion
  • Provides constructive feedback
  • Manages difficult conversations effectively
  • Presents ideas with confidence
  • Facilitates effective meetings

Strategic Thinking

  • Drives execution of strategic priorities
  • Identifies risks and mitigation strategies
  • Works productively with ambiguous information

Relationship Building

  • Manages cross-functional stakeholders effectively
  • Builds trust and credibility with senior leaders
  • Is viewed as a positive collaborator by peers
  • Defuses tension related to work deliverables
