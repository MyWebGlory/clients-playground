Program Proposal

RxVP Emerging Leader Training Program Proposal for ZS Associates India

50 participants identified with managers and HR
30 finalists selected for the emerging leader cohort
10 mo. selection, coaching, workshops, events, and graduation

Program Roadmap

June2026
  • Work with managers and HR to identify 50 appropriate participants.
  • Create the program invitation outlining the honor of being selected, expected time commitment, application, and program goals.
  • Include a LinkedIn announcement participants can share if selected.
July2026
  • Review applications and select 30 participants plus 9 runners-up.
  • Notify selected emerging leaders and collect acceptances or rejections.
  • Replace any declined spots with runners-up and notify them promptly.
  • Finalize the cohort of 30 and share the list with participants and their managers.
August2026
  • Hold a workshop with the managers of the 30 finalists and have each manager complete a skill development checklist for their participant.
  • Run two introductory workshops for emerging leaders to help the cohort bond and align on participant needs.
  • Most participant workshops will be delivered in two sessions of 15 people each.
September2026
  • Conduct one-on-one coaching assessment sessions with all 30 participants.
  • Form coaching subgroups based on the needs identified in those assessments.
  • Create short video of each participant to compare before and after delivery skills.
October2026
  • Run two participant workshops using role-play to address time management, prioritization, delegation, and the art of saying no.
  • Hold coach meetings to prepare for the one-on-one manager meetings in November.
  • Schedule the November manager meetings.
